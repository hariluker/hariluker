# Brookwood Golf Club — researched course facts (2026-08-08)

Confirmed via multiple independent search sources (see architecture doc process;
scorecard hosts themselves were egress-blocked, so values come from search-indexed
content — confidence moderate-to-good, to be re-verified against the club's
scorecard when directly reachable):

- Address: 10304 Bluffton Rd, Fort Wayne, IN 46809 (Allen County), (260) 747-3136
- Approx. clubhouse coords: 40.9887, -85.1685 (east side of Bluffton Rd,
  north of Fort Wayne International Airport)
- 18-hole regulation course (+ separate 18-hole par-3 course), public daily-fee
- Designer Harry Collis, opened 1925 (some sources say 1926)
- Par 72; back tee = **Blue** (no black tee); Blue total ~6,746 yd
  (GolfLink says 6,716; golf.com says 6,736 — conflict noted)
- **Hole 1: Par 5, 520 yd from Blue tees, handicap 13** — matches the product
  brief's "approximately 520 yards"
- Other hole-1 tee yardages: NOT confirmed (only unverified estimates found)
- A creek meanders through the course, in play on six holes
- Hole 1 routing direction: not stated by any source — to be determined from
  licensed imagery during calibration
